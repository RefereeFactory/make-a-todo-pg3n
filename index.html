    How it works

    1. Type a task and press Add (or hit Enter).
    2. Tap the circle to mark something done — tap again to undo.
    3. Click a task's text to edit it; use the × to remove it.
    4. Filter by All, Active or Done, and tidy up with Clear completed.
    5. Your list is saved to the cloud automatically, so it's there on every visit and every device.
